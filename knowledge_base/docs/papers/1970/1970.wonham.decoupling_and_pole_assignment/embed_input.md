Decoupling and Pole Assignment in Linear Multivariable Systems: A Geometric Approach

Topics include Geometric control, Output decoupling, Pole assignment, Controllability subspaces, Linear multivariable systems, State feedback, Disturbance decoupling.

Wonham and Morse formulate decoupling, disturbance neutralization, and pole assignment for linear multivariable systems in a geometric state-space framework. The paper introduces controllability subspace ideas that make structural feedback synthesis problems more transparent than direct transfer-matrix algebra.

The current interest in linear multivariable control has led to several algebraic results with important applications to system synthesis. In particular, the problem of decoupling of individual system outputs by means of state variable feedback was studied by Rekasius, Falb and Wolovich and Gilbert; the problem of realizing arbitrary pole locations in the closed loop system transfer matrix was investigated by Wonham and Heymann. In the present article, new results are obtained along these lines. In § 3, the problem of neutralizing the effect of disturbances with respect to a specified group of output variables is solved. In § 4, the concept of a controllability subspace is introduced and its relation to pole assignability is investigated. This material is preliminary to the formulation of a general problem of output decoupling in § 5.
