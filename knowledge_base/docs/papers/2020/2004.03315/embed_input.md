Learning Control Barrier Functions from Expert Demonstrations

Inspired by the success of imitation and inverse reinforcement learning in replicating expert behavior through optimal control, we propose a learning based approach to safe controller synthesis based on control barrier functions (CBFs). We consider the setting of a known nonlinear control affine dynamical system and assume that we have access to safe trajectories generated by an expert - a practical example of such a setting would be a kinematic model of a self-driving vehicle with safe trajectories (e.g., trajectories that avoid collisions with obstacles in the environment) generated by a human driver. We then propose and analyze an optimization-based approach to learning a CBF that enjoys provable safety guarantees under suitable Lipschitz smoothness assumptions on the underlying dynamical system. A strength of our approach is that it is agnostic to the parameterization used to represent the CBF, assuming only that the Lipschitz constant of such functions can be efficiently bounded.
