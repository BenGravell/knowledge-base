Subdivision of the Audible Frequency Range into Critical Bands (Frequenzgruppen)

Topics include Audio compression, Psychophysics, Acoustic models, Perceptual metrics.

Introduces the subdivision of the audible frequency range into critical bands, the psychoacoustic structure later associated with the Bark scale. Its importance for compression is foundational: perceptual codecs use critical-band masking behavior to decide which quantization noise and spectral detail can be hidden from listeners.
