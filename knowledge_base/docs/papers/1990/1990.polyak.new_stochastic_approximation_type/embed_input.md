New Stochastic Approximation Type Procedures

Topics include Polyak averaging, Stochastic approximation, Averaged iterates, Stochastic optimization, Asymptotic efficiency, Recursive estimation.

<!-- chunk {"id": "summary-0002", "role": "summary", "section": "Summary", "weight": 2.0} -->

Polyak develops new stochastic-approximation procedures based on averaging the trajectory of iterates rather than relying only on the final iterate. Together with Ruppert's work, this established Polyak-Ruppert averaging as a key technique for improving asymptotic efficiency in stochastic approximation and stochastic gradient methods.

<!-- chunk {"id": "abstract-0003", "role": "abstract", "section": "Abstract", "weight": 2.0} -->

The paper introduces stochastic approximation procedures based on averaging iterates, showing that suitable averaging can improve convergence and asymptotic statistical efficiency.
