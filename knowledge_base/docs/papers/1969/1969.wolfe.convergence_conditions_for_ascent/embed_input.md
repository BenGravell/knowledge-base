Convergence Conditions for Ascent Methods

Topics include Wolfe conditions, Line search, Ascent methods, Descent methods, Nonlinear optimization, Convergence analysis.

Wolfe gives general step-size conditions for ascent and descent methods that guarantee convergence while allowing inexact line searches. These conditions became the Wolfe line-search conditions used throughout nonlinear optimization to balance sufficient improvement with curvature information.

Liberal conditions on the steps of a “descent” method for finding extrema of a function are given; most known results are special cases.
