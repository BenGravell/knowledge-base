Discovery of the Maximum Principle

Topics include Optimal control, Maximum principle, Control history, Pontryagin, Calculus of variations.

Gives a short historical account of how Pontryagin and collaborators discovered the maximum principle in optimal control. The entry is useful as a primary recollection of the intellectual development behind one of the central necessary conditions in control theory.

A short history of the discovery of the maximum principle in optimal control theory by L. S. Pontryagin and his associates is presented.
