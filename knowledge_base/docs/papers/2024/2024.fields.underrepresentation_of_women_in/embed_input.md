Underrepresentation of Women in Robotics Research [Women in Engineering]

<!-- chunk {"id": "abstract-0002", "role": "abstract", "section": "Abstract", "weight": 2.0} -->

Computer science (CS) and engineering research both have large and well-documented gender diversity gaps. In fact, previous studies have reported that the overall CS female author ratio (FAR) is only in the range of 16%–26%. As shown in Table 1, recent evidence shows that this number varies significantly among CS subfields, ranging from as high as 42% in CS education to as low as 8% in theory and algorithms,. Furthermore, while recent work has shown that the diversity in conference leadership has increased substantially over recent years and that the state of gender diversity in marine robotics ranges from 7% to 44% across various countries in Europe, there has not been a comprehensive study analyzing the current state of gender diversity across the broader overall field of robotics.
