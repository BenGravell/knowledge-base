An Acceleration of Newton's Method: Super-Halley Method

Topics include Super-halley method, Newton's method, Root finding, Banach spaces, Third-order methods, Convergence analysis.

<!-- chunk {"id": "summary-0002", "role": "summary", "section": "Summary", "weight": 2.0} -->

Derives the Super-Halley method as a third-order acceleration of Newton's method using convexity arguments, then analyzes it for nonlinear equations in Banach spaces. The paper contributes convergence, existence, uniqueness, and error-estimate conditions for the accelerated iteration.

<!-- chunk {"id": "abstract-0003", "role": "abstract", "section": "Abstract", "weight": 2.0} -->

From a study of the convexity we give an acceleration for Newton's method and obtain a new third order method. Then we use this method for solving non-linear equations in Banach spaces, establishing conditions on convergence, existence and uniqueness of solution, as well as error estimates
