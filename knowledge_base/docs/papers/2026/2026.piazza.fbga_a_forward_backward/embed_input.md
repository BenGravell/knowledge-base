FBGA: A Forward-Backward Method for Online Time-Optimal Velocity Planning with Generic Acceleration Constraints

Topics include Time-optimal planning, Speed planning, Acceleration constraints, Vehicle dynamics, Autonomous racing, Real-time planning, Optimal control.

<!-- chunk {"id": "summary-0002", "role": "summary", "section": "Summary", "weight": 2.0} -->

Extends forward-backward velocity planning to generic, speed-dependent acceleration envelopes such as g-g-v diagrams. FBGA closely matches optimal-control lap times while reducing full-lap computation to milliseconds, making high-fidelity speed planning practical inside online sampling-based planners.

<!-- chunk {"id": "abstract-0003", "role": "abstract", "section": "Abstract", "weight": 2.0} -->

We present FBGA, a new algorithm for time-optimal velocity planning under generic acceleration constraints. By extending previous forward-backward approaches to handle custom acceleration constraints, our FBGA matches the accuracy of optimal control baselines while being up to three orders of magnitude faster.
