Solution of Systems of Linear Equations by Minimized Iterations

Topics include Lanczos method, Minimized iterations, Krylov subspace methods, Iterative linear solvers, Linear systems, Numerical linear algebra, Large-scale computation.

Lanczos presents a minimized-iteration scheme for solving large systems of linear algebraic equations through successively improved approximations. The work is an early Krylov-subspace formulation and an important companion to conjugate-gradient methods, especially for understanding how orthogonalization and polynomial approximations can make large linear systems tractable.

A simple algorithm is described which is well adapted to the effective solution of large systems of linear algebraic equations by a succession of well-convergent approximations.
