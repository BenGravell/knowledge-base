Efficient Estimators from a Slowly Converging Robbins-Monro Process

Topics include Polyak-Ruppert averaging, Stochastic approximation, Robbins-monro process, Asymptotic efficiency, Averaged iterates, Statistical estimation.

Ruppert develops iterate averaging for stochastic approximation, showing that averaging can recover asymptotically efficient estimators even when the underlying Robbins-Monro sequence converges slowly. Together with Polyak's related work, this became the Polyak-Ruppert averaging principle used in stochastic optimization and online estimation.

This paper shows how averaging the iterates of a slowly converging Robbins-Monro procedure can produce efficient estimators under suitable regularity conditions.
