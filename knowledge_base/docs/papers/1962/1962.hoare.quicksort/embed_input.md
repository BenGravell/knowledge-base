Quicksort

Topics include Quicksort, Sorting algorithms, Divide and conquer, In-place sorting, Random-access memory, Algorithm analysis.

Hoare introduces Quicksort, an in-place divide-and-conquer sorting algorithm built around partitioning a random-access array. The paper is historically central because Quicksort combines excellent average-case speed, modest storage needs, and implementation simplicity, making it one of the most durable practical sorting algorithms.

A description is given of a new method of sorting in the random-access store of a computer. The method compares very favourably with other known methods in speed, in economy of storage, and in ease of programming. Certain refinements of the method, which may be useful in the optimization of inner loops, are described in the second part of the paper.
