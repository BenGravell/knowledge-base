A Universal Construction of Artstein's Theorem on Nonlinear Stabilization

Topics include Sontag formula, Control Lyapunov functions, Nonlinear stabilization, Artstein theorem, Feedback control, Nonlinear control, Lyapunov methods.

<!-- chunk {"id": "summary-0002", "role": "summary", "section": "Summary", "weight": 2.0} -->

Sontag gives a universal feedback construction that turns suitable control Lyapunov functions into stabilizing nonlinear controllers. The formula made Artstein's existence theorem constructive and became a standard tool in nonlinear stabilization and control-Lyapunov-function design.

<!-- chunk {"id": "abstract-0003", "role": "abstract", "section": "Abstract", "weight": 2.0} -->

This note presents an explicit proof of the theorem - due to Artstein - which states that the existence of a smooth control-Lyapunov function implies smooth stabilizability. Moreover, the result is extended to the real-analytic and rational cases as well. The proof uses a 'universal' formula given by an algebraic function of Lie derivatives; this formula originates in the solution of a simple Riccati equation.
