Idempotent One-Sided Approximation of Median Smoothers

Topics include Signal filtering, Nonlinear smoothing, Median smoothers, LULU smoothing, Idempotent filters, Rank filters, Outlier removal, Lattice methods, Time series.

<!-- chunk {"id": "summary-0002", "role": "summary", "section": "Summary", "weight": 2.0} -->

Introduces one-sided idempotent smoothers that bound and approximate median smoothers while being easier to analyze algebraically. The paper is an early step toward LULU theory, framing nonlinear time-series denoising through ordered lattice operators rather than only through running medians.

<!-- chunk {"id": "abstract-0003", "role": "abstract", "section": "Abstract", "weight": 2.0} -->

There has been considerable interest recently in nonlinear smoothing algorithms for handling outliers in time series. The simpler algorithms are predominantly chosen to be rank-based selectors, and concatenations of these. Analytical investigation lags and is considered difficult, so that theoretical understanding seems inadequate. It may be that a lattice is a useful structure for investigating and comparing smoothers, and a few related simple selectors are defined and investigated for this purpose.
