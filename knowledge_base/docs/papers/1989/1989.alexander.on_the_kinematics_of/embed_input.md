On the Kinematics of Wheeled Mobile Robots

Topics include Wheeled mobile robots, Robot kinematics, Nonholonomic constraints, Mobile robot control, Vehicle models, Motion constraints, Robotics.

<!-- chunk {"id": "summary-0002", "role": "summary", "section": "Summary", "weight": 2.0} -->

Alexander and Maddocks analyze the kinematics of wheeled mobile robots, emphasizing the nonholonomic rolling constraints imposed by wheel contacts. The paper is a useful geometric reference for classifying wheeled platforms and understanding which instantaneous motions are feasible.

<!-- chunk {"id": "abstract-0003", "role": "abstract", "section": "Abstract", "weight": 2.0} -->

A wheeled mobile robot is here modelled as a planar rigid body that rides on an arbitrary number of wheels. The rela tionship between the rigid body motion of the robot and the steering and drive rates of wheels is developed. In particular, conditions are obtained that guarantee that rolling without skidding or sliding can occur. Explicit differential equations are derived to describe the rigid body motions that arise in such ideal rolling trajectories. The simplest wheel configura tion that permits access of arbitrary rigid-body motions is determined. Then the question of slippage due to misalign ment of the wheels is investigated by minimization of a nonsmooth convex dissipation functional that is derived from Coulomb's Law offriction. It is shown that this minimization principle is equivalent to the construction of quasi-static motions. Examples are presented to illustrate the models.
