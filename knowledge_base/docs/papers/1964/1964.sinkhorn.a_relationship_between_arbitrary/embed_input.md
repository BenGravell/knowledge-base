A Relationship between Arbitrary Positive Matrices and Doubly Stochastic Matrices

Topics include Sinkhorn scaling, Matrix scaling, Doubly stochastic matrices, Positive matrices, Diagonal scaling, Matrix analysis.

<!-- chunk {"id": "summary-0002", "role": "summary", "section": "Summary", "weight": 2.0} -->

Sinkhorn proves that positive square matrices have a canonical relationship to doubly stochastic matrices through positive diagonal scaling. The result became the basis for Sinkhorn scaling and later alternating row-column normalization methods used in matrix balancing, optimal transport, statistics, and machine learning.

<!-- chunk {"id": "abstract-0003", "role": "abstract", "section": "Abstract", "weight": 2.0} -->

The author has shown that corresponding to each positive square matrix A, that is, every a_ij > 0, is a unique doubly stochastic matrix of the form D_1 A D_2, where the D_i are diagonal matrices with positive diagonals. This note gives a further relationship between arbitrary positive matrices and doubly stochastic matrices.
