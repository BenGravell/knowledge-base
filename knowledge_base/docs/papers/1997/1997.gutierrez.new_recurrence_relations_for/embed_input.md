New Recurrence Relations for Chebyshev Method

Topics include Chebyshev method, Root finding, Banach spaces, Recurrence relations, Iterative methods, Convergence analysis.

<!-- chunk {"id": "summary-0002", "role": "summary", "section": "Summary", "weight": 2.0} -->

Develops recurrence-relation conditions for convergence of Chebyshev's method in Banach spaces and compares them with earlier sufficient conditions. The contribution is mainly analytical, refining how convergence guarantees for high-order root-finding methods can be expressed.

<!-- chunk {"id": "abstract-0003", "role": "abstract", "section": "Abstract", "weight": 2.0} -->

New conditions on the convergence of the Chebyshev method in Banach spaces are stated by using recurrence relations. The results are compared with the ones obtained by other authors.
