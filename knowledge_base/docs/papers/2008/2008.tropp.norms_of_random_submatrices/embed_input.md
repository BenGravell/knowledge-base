Norms of Random Submatrices and Sparse Approximation

<!-- chunk {"id": "abstract-0002", "role": "abstract", "section": "Abstract", "weight": 2.0} -->

Many problems in the theory of sparse approximation require bounds on operator norms of a random submatrix drawn from a fixed matrix. The purpose of this Note is to collect estimates for several different norms that are most important in the analysis of ℓ 1 minimization algorithms. Several of these bounds have not appeared in detail.
