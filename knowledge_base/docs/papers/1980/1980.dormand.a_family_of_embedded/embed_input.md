A Family of Embedded Runge-Kutta Formulae

Topics include Dormand-Prince method, Runge-Kutta methods, Embedded methods, Ordinary differential equation, Adaptive step size, Numerical integration, Error estimation.

Dormand and Prince derive embedded fifth/fourth-order Runge-Kutta formulae with small fifth-order truncation terms and extended stability regions. The Dormand-Prince pair became one of the standard adaptive ODE solvers because it supplies an efficient higher-order solution and an embedded error estimate for step-size control.

A family of embedded Runge-Kutta formulae RK5 are derived. From these are presented formulae which have (a) 'small' principal truncation terms in the fifth order and (b) extended regions of absolute stability.
