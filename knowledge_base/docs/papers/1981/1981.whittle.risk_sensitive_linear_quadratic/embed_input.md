Risk-sensitive Linear/quadratic/gaussian Control

Topics include Risk-sensitive control, Linear quadratic Gaussian, Linear quadratic Gaussian control, Risk aversion, Kalman filtering, Separation theorem, Stochastic control.

Whittle modifies the standard LQG objective from expected cost to a risk-sensitive criterion with a scalar risk-aversion parameter. The paper shows that linear Markov control, Kalman-style estimation, certainty-equivalence-like structure, and a form of separation survive under the modified objective, extending Jacobson's earlier risk-sensitive control work.

The conventional linear/quadratic/Gaussian assumptions are modified in that minimisation of the expectation of cost G defined by is replaced by minimisation of the criterion function. The scalar –θ is a measure of risk-aversion. It is shown that modified versions of certainty equivalence and the separation theorem still hold, that optimal control is still linear Markov, and state estimate generated by a version of the Kalman filter. There are also various new features, remarked upon in Sections 5 and 7. The paper generalises earlier work of Jacobson.
